PM Desk privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What PM Desk does

PM Desk is a private assistant that reads email sent to one project Gmail account, drafts replies for the account owner to review, and sends approved replies from that account.

Google user data

PM Desk uses Gmail API access with two permissions: read-only access to the project mailbox, and permission to send email from it. PM Desk uses this data only to provide the drafting and sending features described above.

PM Desk does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to train AI models, and does not transfer it to anyone except as needed to provide these features. PM Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data goes

Email text may be sent to an AI model provider to draft replies. Drafts are shown to the account owner in a private Telegram chat before anything is sent. Nothing is sent to a vendor without the owner's approval.

Storage

Mail content and drafts are stored on the owner's own computer and server. Access tokens are stored with restricted permissions and are used only by the services that need them.

Deleting data

You can revoke PM Desk's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access. To request deletion of data PM Desk holds, contact the address below.
